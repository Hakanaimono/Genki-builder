🏃 Motion sentences: 行く / 来る / 帰る / 出かける use に (or へ) to mark the destination → 「[place]に 行きます」. Compare: action verbs use で for the location of an action.
📍 Existence sentences: います (people & animals) and あります (things) use に to mark location → 「[place]に います/あります」.
⚠️ Unlike action verbs (で) and motion verbs (に = destination), に here means where something/someone exists.
